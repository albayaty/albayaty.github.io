I proudly belong to a well-known family of teachers, engineers, physicians, lawyers, and military officers, in Iraq. My family has descended for more than 14 centuries from Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib (601 – 661 C.E.), who was the first cousin and son-in-law of the Islamic Prophet Muhammad and ruled as the fourth Caliph of Islam (656 – 661 C.E.).
