Our heritage proudly belongs to the very well-known family of teachers, engineers, physicians, lawyers, and military officers in Iraq. This family heritage has descended for more than 14 centuries from Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib (601–661 CE), who was the first cousin and son-in-law of the Islamic Prophet Muhammad and ruled as the fourth Caliph of Islam (656–661 CE).
