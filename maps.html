Quantum Circuit Simulators

Our quantum circuit simulators are efficiently designed and built as standalone HTML files that can be downloaded and run in any browser, without installing additional software. Notice that there may be visual glitches and misoperations in these simulators. If you observe any, please let us know by reaching out to "albayaty@pdx.edu". These quantum circuit simulators are intended for academic and non-commercial use, as open-source projects protected by The MIT License.

[1]  Ali Al-Bayaty. 3-Input Ternary (Qutrits) Quantum Circuit Simulator. (2025). GitHub.com. [Online]. Available: https://albayaty.github.io/sw/ternary-qc-sim.html

[2]  Ali Al-Bayaty. 5-Input Hybrid (Qubits+Qutrits) Quantum Circuit Simulator. (2025). GitHub.com. [Online]. Available: https://albayaty.github.io/sw/hybrid-qc-sim.html
